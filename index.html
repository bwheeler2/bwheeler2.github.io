About Me

I’m a system administrator in Baton Rouge, LA, supporting faculty, researchers, students, and staff at LSU’s Center for Computation & Technology (CCT) and partner units in the Office of Research & Economic Development (ORED).

My work covers the full stack: RHEL and Windows servers, Active Directory and SSSD integration, Hyper-V and VMware virtualization, backup and storage platforms, web and database services, and the desktops, laptops, and labs people rely on to teach and do research.

Starting in desktop support taught me that infrastructure only matters if it works for the person in front of the computer. That still shapes how I work: careful changes, clear documentation, and plain-language communication.

  • Infrastructure: Linux & Windows servers, clusters, and VMs

  • Support: desktops, laptops, labs, and specialized applications

How I Work

The habits I bring to every change, migration, and support request.

Test on one before many

Validate changes on an isolated, reversible test system before rolling them out more widely.

Check dependencies before removing anything

Inventory what relies on a system and use reversible shutdown trials before retiring it.

Write it down

Keep runbooks, ticket notes, and handoff documents current so the next person can pick up the work.

Explain it plainly

Give faculty, staff, and students clear, nontechnical updates about what is changing and what they need to do.
